# Roadmap

What could come next. None of it is promised; all of it is open to contributors. Comment on an issue or start a [discussion](https://github.com/nrzz/claude-code-team-sync/discussions) before a large change.

- Walkthroughs for GitLab, Azure DevOps and Bitbucket hubs, with screenshots.
- `claude-team prune --older-than 90d` to remove old shared sessions from a hub.
- A `--since` filter for `list` and `search`.
- A recorded check of `claude --resume <file> --fork-session` on a real imported session (volunteers with two accounts welcome).
